# Signatures

Recurring small details, one line each. They make a scene ours. Source in
brackets; **(proposed)** ones need Lucas's OK.

- **The Kodachrome stripe**: red, yellow, green, blue, equal bands, square ends; a thin rule (`--stripe`) or a small badge, never a fill. At most once a scene. [store]
- **Film-leader eyebrow**: an uppercase label, Outfit 600, tracked `--tr-label`, above a headline. [store]
- **Two cases**: headlines in sentence case; product names, versions and eyebrows uppercase and tracked; nothing else uppercase. [store]
- **Tabular numerals** for every number; versions as `v5.0`. [store]
- **Warm everything**: neutrals, shadows and lines are mixed from warm black or warm cream, never neutral grey or blue-black. [store]
- **Honest ground**: inside a figure the ground is `--viz-ground` (true neutral); warm chrome stops at the figure's edge. [Lab]
- **Data colours exactly as computed**: swatches and gamut points never take a token colour. [Lab]
- **Annotate on the mark, no legends**; R/G/B traces in `--viz-r/g/b`. [Lab + HyperFrames data notes]
- **Develop-in** as the signature entrance for photographs. (proposed)
- **A frame line** (thin `--leader-cream` rule) where film frames meet in a film-advance move. (proposed)
- **Stripe closes the video**: the last frame ends on the stripe rule, like the store's hero bottom edge. (proposed)

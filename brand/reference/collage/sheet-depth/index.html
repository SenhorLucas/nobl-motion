Do

  • Anchor every accent to a feature, a label or a data point.
  • Light comes from a light source: lens, flash, lamp.
  • Scales measure real values and say their unit.
  • Leave paper empty. One hero, one field.

Don't

  • Decorative arrows, ticks, dots or squares.
  • Newsprint or found text. We explain, we don't report.
  • Two circles, two arrows or two highlights in a frame.
  • Crossfades between scenes.

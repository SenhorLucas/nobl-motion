## Purpose
A sample of the collage system in motion: how analog photography works, in four beats.

## Beats
- 0.0s — Expose: Rolleiflex on its circle, light at the taking lens. Push through the lens.
- 3.2s — Develop: negative strip placed; keeper mark on frame 24. Paper slide.
- 6.4s — Enlarge: test strip placed band by band; keeper mark on 16s. Match cut: band -> blue block.
- 9.2s — Print: the photographer's print on the block. Hold.
